Adds Unlock + pin, which opens the door and sets a Mova reminder.

The watch app, the tile, and a new SmartPlus Unlock + Pin complication can
unlock and ask Mova on the phone to pin "Bring scooter in". The reminder
alerts after 10 minutes and every 10 minutes after that until it is marked
Done. This needs Mova 7.9.0 or later on the phone.
